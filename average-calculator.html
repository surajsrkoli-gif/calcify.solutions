📖 Understanding Averages

  • Mean (Average): Sum of all values divided by count. Most common measure of central tendency.
  • Median: Middle value when data is sorted. Less affected by outliers.
  • Mode: Value that appears most frequently. Can be multiple modes.
  • Range: Difference between maximum and minimum values.

⚙️ Formulas

Mean = Σx / n
Median = middle value (if n odd) or average of two middle values (if n even)
Mode = value(s) with highest frequency
Range = Max(x) - Min(x)
