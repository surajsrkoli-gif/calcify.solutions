📖 What is a Retirement Calculator?

A retirement calculator helps you estimate how much money you will need to maintain your desired lifestyle after retirement and how much you should save monthly. It considers inflation, expected returns, current expenses, and life expectancy to give you a realistic financial roadmap.

⚙️ How the Calculation Works

The calculator follows three steps:

  1. Future Monthly Expense: Your current monthly expense inflated up to retirement age: FV = Current Expense × (1 + inflation)(retirement age - current age)
  2. Corpus Needed at Retirement: A sum that can generate the inflated monthly expense for the rest of your life (post-retirement period). Using the present value of annuity formula (assuming withdrawals at beginning of each month).
  3. Monthly Savings Required: Amount to save each month from now until retirement to accumulate the target corpus, using the future value of annuity formula (monthly compounding).

The result shows the corpus needed, monthly savings, and expected monthly income in today's and future terms.

📐 Understanding Your Results

  • Corpus Required: The lump sum you need at retirement age to fund your expenses for life.
  • Monthly Savings Required: How much you should invest each month (from today until retirement) to reach that corpus.
  • Monthly Retirement Income: The inflation-adjusted monthly amount you can withdraw during retirement (shown in today's purchasing power and future nominal value).

Start saving early to reduce the monthly burden and benefit from compounding.

✅ Why Use a Retirement Calculator?

  • Set realistic retirement goals based on your lifestyle.
  • Understand the impact of inflation on your future needs.
  • Adjust your savings rate or retirement age to see trade-offs.
  • Plan for tax-efficient retirement instruments (NPS, PPF, mutual funds).
  • Free, instant, and no personal data stored.

📜 Importance of Retirement Planning

With increasing life expectancy and reducing joint family support, retirement planning has become critical. The earlier you start, the less you need to save monthly. A rule of thumb is to aim for a corpus that is 30-40 times your annual expenses. Modern retirement calculators empower individuals to take control of their financial future with data-driven insights.
