📖 What is an FD Calculator?

A Fixed Deposit (FD) calculator helps you estimate the maturity amount and total interest earned on your fixed deposit investment. It uses the principal amount, interest rate, tenure, and compounding frequency to provide accurate projections, helping you compare FD schemes from different banks.

⚙️ How FD Interest is Calculated

The calculator uses the compound interest formula:

A = P × (1 + r/n)n×t

Where A = Maturity amount, P = Principal, r = Annual interest rate (in decimal), n = Number of compounding periods per year, t = Tenure in years. Total interest = A - P. The effective annual yield is the actual return considering compounding frequency.

📐 Understanding Your FD Returns

  • Total Interest Earned: The amount you earn on your deposit over the tenure.
  • Maturity Amount: The final amount you receive (Principal + Interest).
  • Effective Yield: The actual annualized return after considering compounding frequency.

For example, an FD with 7.5% p.a. compounded quarterly yields an effective rate of approximately 7.71%.

✅ Why Use an FD Calculator?

  • Compare fixed deposit schemes from different banks and NBFCs.
  • Plan your savings for short-term or long-term goals.
  • Understand the impact of compounding frequency (monthly vs. quarterly vs. annually).
  • Decide between cumulative and non-cumulative FD options.
  • Free, instant, and completely private — no personal data required.

📜 Fixed Deposits in India

Fixed deposits are one of the oldest and safest investment options in India. Banks and post offices offer FDs with tenures ranging from 7 days to 10 years. Senior citizens typically get higher interest rates (0.5% extra). Since the 1990s, online FD calculators have made it easy for depositors to project returns and choose the best deposit options.
