📖 What is Pace?

Pace is the time it takes to cover a unit of distance — usually minutes per kilometer (min/km) or minutes per mile (min/mile). It's the standard metric for runners to gauge effort and performance. A lower pace means faster running.

⚙️ How It Works

Pace = Time / Distance
Speed = Distance / Time
Time = Pace × Distance
Distance = Time / Pace
Our calculator handles conversions between metric and imperial units, and formats time in hours, minutes, seconds.

📊 Typical Running Paces

  • Elite marathon: ~3:00-3:30 min/km
  • Intermediate 5k: ~5:00-6:00 min/km
  • Recreational jogging: ~6:30-8:00 min/km
  • Walking: ~10:00-12:00 min/km
