📖 How to Use

Date Difference: Enter two dates to find the exact number of days, weeks, approximate months and years between them.

Add/Subtract: Select a starting date, quantity, unit (days, weeks, months, years), and operation (add/subtract) to get the resulting date.

⚙️ Calculation Notes

All date arithmetic uses UTC to avoid timezone issues. Months and years in difference are approximate based on average days per month (30.44) and per year (365.25). For exact month/year differences, add/subtract uses calendar logic (e.g., Jan 31 + 1 month = Feb 28/29).

📐 Example

  • From Jan 1, 2025 to Dec 31, 2025: 364 days, 52 weeks, ~11.96 months, ~1 year.
  • Add 30 days to Jan 15, 2025 → Feb 14, 2025.
  • Subtract 1 month from Mar 31, 2025 → Feb 28, 2025.
