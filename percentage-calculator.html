📖 What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. It is denoted using the percent sign (%). For example, 45% means 45 out of 100. Percentages are used to compare proportions, calculate discounts, express interest rates, and much more.

🧮 Percentage Formulas

• % of a number: (Percentage / 100) × Number
• What percent: (Part / Whole) × 100%
• % change: ((New - Old) / |Old|) × 100%

Our calculator applies these formulas instantly for all three scenarios.

📜 History of Percentages

The word "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum" meaning "by the hundred." Ancient Romans used fractions of 100 for taxation, but the modern percentage notation became widespread in the 15th century with the rise of commerce and banking. Today, percentages are fundamental in finance, statistics, and everyday life.
