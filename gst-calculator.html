📖 What is GST?

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced multiple cascading taxes (VAT, service tax, excise duty) on July 1, 2017, under the "One Nation, One Tax" principle. GST has four main slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%.

⚙️ How GST is Calculated

GST Exclusive (Add GST): If you have the original price, GST amount = Original × (Rate/100). Total = Original + GST. CGST = SGST = GST/2.

GST Inclusive (Remove GST): If total amount includes GST, Original = Total / (1 + Rate/100). GST = Total - Original. CGST = SGST = GST/2.

Exclusive GST: GST Amount = (Original × Rate) / 100
Inclusive GST: Original Amount = Total × 100 / (100 + Rate)

📐 Understanding GST Components (CGST + SGST)

  • CGST (Central GST): Collected by the Central Government for intra-state supplies.
  • SGST (State GST): Collected by the State Government for intra-state supplies.
  • IGST (Integrated GST): For inter-state supplies (not shown here, but CGST+SGST = total GST for intra-state).

Our calculator splits total GST equally into CGST and SGST for intra-state transactions (default).

📜 GST in India

GST was introduced through the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016, and implemented on July 1, 2017. It is a destination-based consumption tax. Essential goods have lower rates (0%, 5%), while luxury items attract 28% plus cess. GST has simplified indirect taxation and increased transparency. Online GST calculators have become essential tools for businesses, accountants, and consumers to verify tax amounts.
