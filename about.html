📖 Our Story

It started with a frustrating afternoon. I needed a quick EMI calculation, but every website I visited was plastered with pop‑up ads, slow scripts, or required me to sign up. The few that worked looked like they hadn't been updated since 1999. Sound familiar?

That same week, a friend asked me, "Why is there no simple, clean, and accurate SIP calculator?" That was the spark. I gathered a small group of developers, designers, and finance enthusiasts who felt the same. We realized that the world deserves better — a place where you can calculate a mortgage, check your BMI, or generate a random password without feeling like you're being tracked or tricked.

“We built calcify.solutions for our own families, for students, for freelancers, and for anyone who hates cluttered interfaces. Every calculator is hand‑coded with care, runs entirely in your browser, and respects your privacy.”

After six months of late nights, coffee, and far too many edge cases, we launched with 40 calculators. Today, we've grown to over 70 tools, from finance and health to science and utility. And we're not stopping.

🎯 Our Mission & Values

🔒 Privacy by default

We don't store your numbers. We don't track you. All calculations happen inside your own device — no servers, no logs. You can use our tools even with airplane mode on (once loaded).

⚡ Speed is a feature

Every calculator is optimized for instant response. No loading spinners, no unnecessary animations that slow you down. Just pure, lightweight JavaScript and clean CSS.

📐 Accuracy you can trust

We use industry‑standard formulas (Mifflin‑St Jeor for BMR, US Navy method for body fat, Frankfurter API for exchange rates, etc.). You can even check our step‑by‑step breakdowns.

🧠 Designed for humans

Glassmorphism, soft glows, and intuitive layouts aren't just for show — they reduce cognitive load and make complex calculations feel easy. We obsess over spacing, font sizes, and mobile touch targets.

💸 Forever free

No premium tiers, no "pro" accounts. We believe essential tools should never be paywalled. (We may show unobtrusive ads to keep the lights on, but we'll never sell your data or lock basic features.)

📜 Legal & Transparency

Calcify.solutions is provided "as is," but we stand behind our work. Our calculators are for informational purposes — we're not financial advisors or doctors, but we strive for perfection. If you find a bug or an inaccurate formula, please tell us. We'll fix it within 48 hours.

We use Frankfurter API for currency rates (European Central Bank data) and CurrencyAPI for backup. No third‑party cookies, no tracking scripts, no Google Analytics. Our only external requests are for exchange rates — everything else runs locally.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and is 100% static. You can even download the entire repository and run it offline.
