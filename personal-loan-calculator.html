📖 What is a Personal Loan Calculator?

A personal loan calculator helps you estimate the monthly EMI, total interest, and total repayment amount for an unsecured personal loan. It's an essential tool for budgeting before you apply, allowing you to compare different loan amounts, interest rates, and tenures.

⚙️ How the Personal Loan EMI is Calculated

The calculator uses the standard EMI formula based on the reducing balance method, which is used by all banks and NBFCs:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n – 1)

Where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = loan tenure in months (years × 12). The result gives you a fixed monthly payment that includes both principal and interest.

📐 Understanding Your EMI Components

Each EMI payment consists of two parts:

  • Principal repayment: Reduces your outstanding loan amount.
  • Interest payment: Cost of borrowing calculated on remaining principal.

Initially, interest forms a larger portion, but over time, principal repayment increases. Our calculator instantly shows you the total interest and overall repayment.

✅ Why Use a Personal Loan Calculator?

  • Plan your monthly budget before taking a loan.
  • Compare offers from different lenders (interest rates vary from 8% to 24%).
  • Decide the optimal tenure — shorter tenure means higher EMI but lower total interest.
  • Avoid surprises: know exactly how much you will repay in total.
  • Free, instant, and completely private — no personal data needed.

Today, a personal loan EMI calculator is a must-have tool before signing any loan agreement, helping millions make informed financial decisions.
