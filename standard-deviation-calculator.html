📖 What is Standard Deviation?

Standard deviation measures the spread of data points from the mean. A low standard deviation means data points are close to the mean; a high standard deviation indicates data is spread out. Population SD (σ) uses n, while sample SD (s) uses n-1 for unbiased estimation.

⚙️ Formulas

  • Mean (μ or x̄): Σx / n
  • Population Variance (σ²): Σ(x - μ)² / n
  • Population SD (σ): √σ²
  • Sample Variance (s²): Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)
  • Sample SD (s): √s²

📐 Understanding the Difference

Population standard deviation is used when you have all data points from a population. Sample standard deviation is used when you have a sample from a larger population (n-1 provides an unbiased estimate).
