📖 How to Use

Select your date of birth. Optionally, choose a custom "as of" date (default is today). The calculator will compute your exact age in years, months, days, and other units, plus the countdown to your next birthday.

⚙️ Calculation Method

Age is calculated using precise date difference accounting for leap years and month lengths. The age in years, months, days shows the actual calendar difference. Total days, weeks, hours, minutes, seconds are also displayed.

📐 Birthday Countdown

The countdown shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds until your next birthday. If today is your birthday, it shows a celebratory message.
