📖 What is ROI?

Return on Investment (ROI) is a performance measure used to evaluate the efficiency or profitability of an investment. It compares the gain (profit) relative to the cost (initial investment). ROI is expressed as a percentage and is widely used in finance, business, and personal investing.

⚙️ How ROI is Calculated

Simple ROI (%) = (Final Value - Initial Investment) / Initial Investment × 100

Annualized ROI (CAGR) = [(Final Value / Initial Investment)(1 / Years) - 1] × 100

If your investment grew from ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 over 3 years, the simple ROI is 50%, and the annualized ROI (CAGR) is approximately 14.47% per year.

📐 Understanding Your Results

  • Total Profit: Absolute gain from your investment (Final Value - Initial Investment).
  • Simple ROI: Total return over the entire investment period, ignoring time.
  • Annualized ROI (CAGR): Average annual return, smoothing out volatility. Essential for comparing investments with different time horizons.

Annualized ROI is more meaningful for long-term investments because it accounts for the time value of money.

✅ Why Use an ROI Calculator?

  • Evaluate potential investments (stocks, mutual funds, real estate).
  • Compare different business projects or marketing campaigns.
  • Track your personal portfolio performance over time.
  • Set realistic return expectations before investing.
  • Free, instant, and no personal data required.

📜 Limitations of ROI

Simple ROI does not consider the time period — a 50% return over 1 year is far better than over 5 years. That's why annualized ROI (CAGR) is important. Also, ROI may not account for risk, inflation, or taxes. Use it as one of several metrics in your investment analysis.
