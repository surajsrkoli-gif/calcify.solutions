📖 How to Use

Select a unit category (Length, Mass, Volume, Temperature, Area). Choose the "From" and "To" units, enter a value, and the converted result appears instantly. Use the swap button (⇄) to reverse the units.

⚙️ Supported Conversions

  • Length: meters, km, cm, mm, miles, yards, feet, inches
  • Mass: kg, g, mg, lb, oz
  • Volume: liters, mL, gallons, quarts, pints, cups
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Area: m², km², ft², yd², acres, hectares
