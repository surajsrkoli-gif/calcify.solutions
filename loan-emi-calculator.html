📐 EMI Formula & How It Works

The Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is calculated using the standard amortization formula:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n – 1)
Where: P = principal loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = number of monthly installments (tenure in months)

Example: Loan amount $25,000, annual rate 10.5%, tenure 5 years (60 months). Monthly rate = 10.5/12/100 = 0.00875. EMI = 25000 × 0.00875 × (1.00875)^60 / ((1.00875)^60 – 1) ≈ $537.45. Total interest ≈ $7,247.

This calculator works for any type of loan: personal, home, car, education, or business loan.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI? EMI is a fixed monthly payment made to a lender to repay a loan, consisting of both principal and interest components.
How does tenure affect EMI? Longer tenure reduces EMI but increases total interest payable. Shorter tenure increases EMI but saves interest.
Can I use this for home loan EMI? Yes, but home loans may have additional fees like processing charges – this calculator gives the basic EMI.
Is there a prepayment penalty? Many loans allow prepayment – check with your lender. This calculator assumes no prepayment.
