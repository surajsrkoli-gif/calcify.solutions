📖 What is Profit Margin?

Profit margin is a measure of profitability. It represents the percentage of revenue that turns into profit. Gross profit margin is calculated as (Profit / Selling Price) × 100. Markup percentage is (Profit / Cost Price) × 100. Both metrics help businesses set prices and evaluate performance.

⚙️ How Calculations Work

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price
Profit Margin (%) = (Profit / Selling Price) × 100
Markup (%) = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100

If your cost price is ₹500 and selling price ₹800, profit = ₹300, profit margin = 37.5%, markup = 60%. This means you added 60% to your cost to set the price, but your margin on sales is 37.5%.

📐 Understanding Your Results

  • Profit Amount: Absolute profit earned per unit or transaction.
  • Gross Profit Margin: The percentage of selling price that is profit. Higher margin means better profitability on sales.
  • Markup Percentage: The percentage added to cost price to determine selling price. Useful for pricing products.

Both metrics are vital for comparing products, tracking business health, and making pricing decisions.

📜 Profit Margin in Business

Profit margins vary widely by industry. Grocery stores operate on thin margins (1-3%), while software companies often have margins above 80%. Understanding your margin is crucial for survival and growth. This calculator helps entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small business owners quickly assess their profitability.
