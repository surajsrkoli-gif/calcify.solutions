Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are your calculators?
All calculators use standard formulas (BMI = kg/m², EMI = P×r×(1+r)ⁿ/((1+r)ⁿ-1), compound interest, etc.) and are tested for precision against manual calculations.
Do I need to create an account to use these calculators?
No – everything is 100% free with no registration required. Just visit the page and start calculating.
Can I use these calculators on my mobile phone?
Yes, fully responsive design with touch-friendly inputs. Works on all modern browsers.
What is the EMI formula?
EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1], where P = principal, R = monthly interest rate, N = number of monthly installments.
How do I calculate my BMI?
BMI = weight (kg) / (height in meters)². Use our BMI calculator for instant results and health category.
