📖 What is an RD Calculator?

A Recurring Deposit (RD) calculator helps you estimate the maturity amount and total interest earned on your recurring deposit. In an RD, you invest a fixed amount every month for a predetermined tenure, and the bank pays compound interest on your monthly deposits.

⚙️ How RD Interest is Calculated

The calculator uses the future value of a series of monthly deposits formula:

M = R × [(1 + r/n)n×t - 1] / (1 - (1 + r/n)-1/n)

Alternatively, a simpler approximation: RD maturity is calculated using the compound interest formula for recurring deposits where each monthly installment earns interest for the remaining tenure. Our calculator accurately computes the final maturity amount considering monthly compounding.

📐 Understanding Your RD Returns

  • Total Invested Amount: Sum of all your monthly deposits over the tenure.
  • Total Interest Earned: The compound interest generated on your recurring deposits.
  • Maturity Amount: The final amount you receive (Principal + Interest).
  • Effective Yield: The actual annualized return after considering compounding frequency.

RDs are ideal for disciplined monthly savers who want guaranteed returns with low risk.

✅ Why Use an RD Calculator?

  • Compare RD schemes from different banks and post offices.
  • Understand the impact of compounding frequency and tenure.
  • Decide how much to save each month to reach your target corpus.
  • Free, instant, and completely private — no personal data required.

📜 Recurring Deposits in India

Recurring deposits are popular among salaried individuals and students to build a savings habit. Banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and post offices offer RD accounts with tenures ranging from 6 months to 10 years. Interest rates are similar to fixed deposits, and senior citizens often get an additional 0.5% p.a. Online RD calculators have made it easy for millions to project their maturity amount and plan their finances.
