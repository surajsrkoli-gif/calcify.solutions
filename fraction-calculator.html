📖 How to Use the Fraction Calculator

Enter the numerator and denominator for both fractions (denominator cannot be zero). Choose an operation (+, -, ×, ÷). The calculator will compute the result in simplified form, show the decimal equivalent, and provide a step-by-step explanation of how the result was obtained.

⚙️ Calculation Rules

  • Addition/Subtraction: Find common denominator, then add/subtract numerators.
  • Multiplication: Multiply numerators, multiply denominators.
  • Division: Multiply first fraction by reciprocal of second.
  • Simplification: Divide numerator and denominator by GCD.

📐 Understanding Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole. The numerator (top) indicates how many parts, the denominator (bottom) indicates total equal parts. Proper fractions have numerator < denominator; improper fractions have numerator ≥ denominator. This calculator also handles whole numbers (denominator = 1).
