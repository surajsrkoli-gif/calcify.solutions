📐 Mortgage Formula & How It Works

The monthly principal & interest (P&I) is calculated using the standard amortization formula:

M = P × [ r(1+r)^n ] / [ (1+r)^n – 1 ]
Where: P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual/12), n = total monthly payments (years × 12)

Example: $300,000 home, 20% down ($60,000 loan), 30 years @ 6.5% → P&I ≈ $1,520. Add property tax ($300/mo), insurance ($100/mo), HOA $0 → total ~$1,920/month.

This calculator also includes optional property tax, home insurance, and HOA fees to give you a realistic PITI + HOA payment estimate.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good mortgage interest rate?
Rates vary by market and credit score. As of 2025, 6-7% is typical for a 30-year fixed. Always compare offers.
Should I include property tax and insurance?
Yes – most lenders require escrow for taxes and insurance, so including them gives a true monthly cost.
What is PMI?
If down payment is less than 20%, you may need Private Mortgage Insurance. This calculator does not include PMI for simplicity – add it manually if needed.
How can I lower my monthly payment?
Increase down payment, choose a longer loan term, or find a lower interest rate.
