📖 What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple index of weight-for-height that is commonly used to classify underweight, overweight, and obesity in adults. It is defined as the weight in kilograms divided by the square of the height in meters (kg/m²). BMI provides a reliable indicator of body fatness for most people.

⚙️ How BMI is Calculated

The formula differs for metric and imperial units:

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))²
Imperial: BMI = (weight (lbs) / (height (in))²) × 703

Our calculator instantly converts between units and gives you the precise BMI value along with the standard WHO classification.

📊 BMI Categories (WHO Standards)

  • Underweight: BMI less than 18.5
  • Normal weight: BMI 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: BMI 25 – 29.9
  • Obesity Class I: BMI 30 – 34.9
  • Obesity Class II: BMI 35 – 39.9
  • Obesity Class III: BMI ≥ 40

✅ Why Calculate Your BMI?

  • Assess if you are in a healthy weight range.
  • Identify potential health risks (heart disease, diabetes, etc.).
  • Set realistic fitness and weight management goals.
  • Track progress over time.
  • Free, quick, and private health check.

📜 Limitations of BMI

While BMI is a useful screening tool, it does not directly measure body fat or account for muscle mass, bone density, or distribution of fat. Athletes or muscular individuals may have a high BMI but low body fat. For a complete health assessment, consult a doctor.
