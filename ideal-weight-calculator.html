📖 What is Ideal Body Weight?

Ideal body weight (IBW) is an estimate of the optimal weight for a person based on height, gender, and sometimes frame size. Different formulas exist for different purposes: Devine (most common for drug dosing), Robinson, and Miller (adjusted for modern populations).

⚙️ How the Formulas Work

Devine Formula (1974):
Men: 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft
Women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft

Robinson Formula (1983):
Men: 52 kg + 1.9 kg per inch over 5 ft
Women: 49 kg + 1.9 kg per inch over 5 ft

Miller Formula (1983):
Men: 56.2 kg + 1.41 kg per inch over 5 ft
Women: 53.1 kg + 1.36 kg per inch over 5 ft

We convert cm to feet/inches for these formulas. Frame adjustment: small frame = -10%, large frame = +10% of the calculated value.

📊 Healthy BMI Range

BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults. For a given height, we calculate the weight range corresponding to these BMI values. For height 175 cm (1.75 m):
Lower limit = 18.5 × (height in m)², Upper limit = 24.9 × (height in m)².

📜 Limitations

Ideal weight formulas do not account for muscle mass, bone density, or body composition. Athletes may weigh more due to muscle. These estimates are starting points — consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
