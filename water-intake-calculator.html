📖 Why Water Intake Matters

Water makes up about 60% of your body weight and is essential for temperature regulation, joint lubrication, nutrient transport, and waste removal. Proper hydration improves cognitive function, physical performance, and overall well-being.

⚙️ How We Calculate

Base Water = Weight (kg) × 35 ml (for adults)
Activity Adjustment = +350 ml per activity level (0 to 4)
Climate Adjustment = +0 to +700 ml based on heat

Base recommendation: 30-40 ml per kg of body weight. Our formula uses 35 ml/kg as baseline, then adds for exercise and hot climates. This aligns with guidelines from the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and Institute of Medicine (IOM).

📊 Activity & Climate Factors

  • Sedentary: +0 ml — Little physical activity.
  • Lightly active: +350 ml — Short walks, light exercise.
  • Moderately active: +700 ml — Regular gym, sports.
  • Very active: +1050 ml — Daily intense workouts.
  • Extremely active: +1400 ml — Athletes, physical labor.
  • Climate: Adds 150–700 ml for hot/humid conditions.

✅ Hydration Tips

  • Drink water before you feel thirsty — thirst indicates mild dehydration.
  • Increase intake during illness, fever, or hot weather.
  • Eat water-rich foods (cucumber, watermelon, oranges).
  • Monitor urine color — pale yellow = well hydrated.
  • Pregnant/breastfeeding women need additional 300-700 ml/day.

📜 Science of Hydration

Research has shown that even 1-2% dehydration can impair cognitive function and physical performance. The "8×8 rule" (eight 8-ounce glasses) is a general starting point, but individual needs vary. Our calculator personalizes your recommendation using evidence-based multipliers.
