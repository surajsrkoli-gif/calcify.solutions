📖 How to Use the Grade Calculator

Points System: Enter the total points you earned and the total points possible for all assignments/tests combined. The calculator gives your percentage grade and letter grade.

Weighted Categories: Add categories (e.g., Homework, Quizzes, Exams), enter the average score (%) for each category and its weight (% of final grade). The calculator computes the weighted average.

⚙️ Letter Grade Scale (Standard)

  • A+: 97-100% | A: 93-96% | A-: 90-92%
  • B+: 87-89% | B: 83-86% | B-: 80-82%
  • C+: 77-79% | C: 73-76% | C-: 70-72%
  • D+: 67-69% | D: 63-66% | D-: 60-62%
  • F: Below 60%

📐 Weighted Grade Formula

Overall Grade = Σ (Category Score × Category Weight) ÷ Σ (Weights). Ensure total weights sum to 100% for accurate results.
