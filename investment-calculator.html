📖 What is an Investment Calculator?

An investment calculator helps you project the growth of your investments over time, considering compound interest and periodic contributions. It's essential for retirement planning, goal-based investing, and understanding the power of compounding.

⚙️ How the Calculation Works

This calculator uses the compound interest formula for lump sum plus future value of a series of monthly contributions:

FV = P × (1 + r/n)n×t + PMT × [((1 + r/n)n×t - 1) / (r/n)] × (1 + r/n)

Where P = initial investment, PMT = monthly contribution, r = annual return rate, n = compounding frequency per year, t = number of years. The result shows the total wealth accumulated, including both contributions and compound returns.

📐 Understanding Your Results

  • Total Invested Amount: Sum of your initial lump sum plus all monthly contributions.
  • Total Interest Earned: The compound returns generated by your investments.
  • Future Value: Your final corpus, ready for withdrawal or reinvestment.

Starting early, contributing regularly, and achieving higher returns dramatically increases the final value due to the magic of compounding.

✅ Why Use This Investment Calculator?

  • Plan for retirement, children's education, or buying a home.
  • Compare different contribution amounts, return rates, and time horizons.
  • Understand the impact of compounding frequency (monthly vs. annual).
  • Set realistic financial goals and track progress.
  • Free, instant, and completely private — no personal data required.
