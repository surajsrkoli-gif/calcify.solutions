Calculate total work hours between start and end times, subtract break durations, and get results in standard (HH:MM) and decimal formats. Includes overtime calculation.

Times are in 12-hour format with AM/PM. Calculator handles overnight shifts (e.g., 9 AM to 5 PM).

📖 How to Use the Hours Calculator

  • Enter start time (hour, minute, AM/PM) and end time.
  • Enter break duration in minutes (e.g., 30 for half-hour lunch).
  • Optionally set your standard workday hours to calculate overtime (default 8 hours).
  • Click "Calculate Hours" to see total hours worked, decimal hours, regular & overtime.
  • Overnight shifts (e.g., 9 PM to 5 AM) are automatically detected.

⚙️ Calculation Logic

Time difference is computed in minutes, then break minutes are subtracted. The result is converted to hours:minutes and decimal hours (e.g., 8.5 hours = 8 hours 30 minutes). Overtime = total hours - standard day (if positive).

📐 Example

Start: 9:00 AM, End: 5:30 PM, Break: 30 minutes → Total worked = 8 hours (5:30 PM - 9:00 AM = 8h30m minus 30m break = 8h). Decimal = 8.00, Overtime = 0 (if standard day = 8h).
