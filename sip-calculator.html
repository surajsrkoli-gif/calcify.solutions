📖 What is a SIP Calculator?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) calculator is an online tool that estimates the maturity value of your monthly mutual fund investments. By entering monthly amount, expected return rate, and investment tenure, you can project future wealth — helping you align with goals like retirement, child education, or buying a home.

⚙️ How SIP Calculator Works

The calculator uses the future value of a series of monthly payments, factoring in compounding returns. It assumes a fixed rate of return and that SIP installments are invested at the beginning of each month. The formula applied is:

FV = P × [ ( (1 + r)n – 1 ) / r ] × (1 + r)

Where P = monthly investment, r = monthly rate of return (annual rate/12), n = total number of months. The tool instantly computes invested amount, total returns, and final corpus.

📐 SIP Formula (Detailed)

M = P × ( ( (1 + i)n – 1 ) / i ) × (1 + i)
M = Maturity amount, P = Monthly SIP amount, i = periodic rate of interest (monthly), n = number of months. This standard formula is used by financial advisors and mutual fund houses.

✅ Benefits of SIP Investment

  • Rupee cost averaging – reduces market timing risk.
  • Power of compounding – small amounts grow significantly over time.
  • Disciplined saving – automated monthly investments.
  • Flexibility – start, stop, or increase SIP anytime.
  • Wealth creation for long-term goals like retirement or travel.

📜 History of SIP Investing

Systematic Investment Plans originated in the early 1990s when mutual funds started offering monthly investment options. In India, SIPs gained massive popularity after the 2000s as retail investors embraced disciplined investing. The rise of online SIP calculators in the 2010s empowered millions to project returns instantly, making financial planning accessible. Today, SIP is the preferred mode for millennials and Gen Z to build long-term wealth with minimal risk.
