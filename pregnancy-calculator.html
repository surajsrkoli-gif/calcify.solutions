📖 How to Use the Pregnancy Calculator

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) or your conception date. The calculator will estimate your due date using Naegele's rule (LMP + 280 days) or conception date + 266 days. It also calculates current gestational age in weeks and days, trimester, and days remaining until delivery.

⚙️ Calculation Method

  • LMP Method: Due Date = LMP + 280 days (40 weeks). This assumes a regular 28-day cycle and ovulation on day 14.
  • Conception Date: Due Date = Conception Date + 266 days (38 weeks). Ovulation/conception typically occurs around day 14 of a 28-day cycle.

For irregular cycles, an ultrasound is more accurate. This calculator provides an estimate.

📊 Pregnancy Timeline

  • First Trimester: Week 1 to Week 12 — Early development, morning sickness common.
  • Second Trimester: Week 13 to Week 27 — Baby grows rapidly, mother feels movement.
  • Third Trimester: Week 28 to Week 40 — Final growth, preparation for delivery.
