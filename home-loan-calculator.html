📖 What is a Home Loan Calculator?

A home loan calculator helps you determine the monthly EMI, total interest, and overall repayment for a housing loan. It's an essential tool for homebuyers to budget effectively and compare different loan offers from banks and housing finance companies.

⚙️ How Home Loan EMI is Calculated

The calculator uses the standard reducing balance EMI formula, which is universally used by lenders:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n – 1)

Where P = principal loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = loan tenure in months (years × 12). The result gives you a fixed monthly payment that includes both principal and interest.

📐 Understanding Your Home Loan EMI

Each EMI payment consists of:

  • Principal repayment: Gradually reduces your outstanding loan amount.
  • Interest payment: Cost of borrowing calculated on the remaining principal.

In the early years, interest forms a larger portion of the EMI. Over time, principal repayment increases. Our calculator instantly shows you total interest and total payment, helping you make informed decisions about tenure and down payment.

✅ Why Use a Home Loan Calculator?

  • Plan your monthly budget before applying for a home loan.
  • Compare different interest rates and tenures from various lenders.
  • Decide the optimal tenure — shorter tenure means higher EMI but lower total interest.
  • Understand the impact of prepayments or part-payments.
  • Free, instant, and completely private — no personal data required.
