📖 What is a Salary Calculator?

A salary calculator (or take-home salary calculator) computes your net in-hand pay after mandatory deductions like Employee Provident Fund (EPF), Professional Tax, and Income Tax. It helps you understand the gap between CTC and what actually credits to your bank account.

⚙️ How Salary is Calculated

Gross Salary = CTC - Employer PF contribution (employer PF is part of CTC but not paid to employee). Deductions = Employee PF + Professional Tax. Taxable Income = Gross Salary - Standard Deduction (₹50,000) - Deductions under 80C/80D (old regime only). Then income tax is computed as per selected regime. In-Hand Salary = Gross Salary - (Employee PF + Professional Tax + Income Tax).

✅ Why Use This Salary Calculator?

  • Know your exact monthly take-home pay before accepting a job offer.
  • Compare the impact of choosing Old vs New Tax Regime on net salary.
  • Plan your investments (80C) to reduce tax liability.
  • Understand how PF contribution reduces your in-hand pay but builds retirement corpus.
  • Free, instant, and no personal data stored.

📜 Important Notes on Deductions

Employee PF is deducted at 12% of basic salary (or a fixed amount for lower salaries). Professional Tax varies by state (e.g., ₹200/month in most states). Standard deduction of ₹50,000 is applied for all salaried individuals. Under Old Regime, you can claim deductions like 80C (up to ₹1.5L), 80D (health insurance), etc. Under New Regime, only standard deduction is allowed.
