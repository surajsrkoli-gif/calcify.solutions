📖 What is PPF?

Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a long-term, government-backed savings scheme in India. It offers compound interest (currently around 7.1% p.a.) with tax benefits under Section 80C (deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh per year). The maturity period is 15 years, extendable in 5-year blocks. Interest is tax-free, making it a popular choice for retirement and tax-saving goals.

⚙️ How PPF Interest is Calculated

PPF interest is calculated monthly on the minimum balance between the 5th and end of each month, but credited annually. The formula for maturity amount with yearly contributions is:

M = P × [(1 + r)n - 1] / r × (1 + r)

For monthly contributions, each deposit compounds for the remaining months. Our calculator handles both yearly lump sum and monthly contributions, using the standard PPF compounding method (annual compounding).

📐 Understanding Your PPF Returns

  • Total Invested Amount: Sum of all yearly or monthly contributions over the tenure.
  • Total Interest Earned: Compound interest generated on your PPF balance.
  • Maturity Amount: The final corpus at the end of the tenure.
  • Effective Yield: The actual annualized return considering compounding.

PPF offers guaranteed returns backed by the Government of India, making it one of the safest long-term investment options.

📜 PPF in India

Introduced in 1968 by the National Savings Institute, PPF has become a cornerstone of retirement planning for millions. The minimum annual deposit is ₹500, and the maximum is ₹1.5 lakh per financial year. Accounts can be opened at post offices or designated banks. PPF calculators have empowered investors to project their maturity corpus and optimize their tax-saving strategies.
