📖 What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal and also on the accumulated interest from previous periods. It's often called "interest on interest" and allows investments to grow exponentially over time. The more frequently interest compounds, the higher the returns.

⚙️ How Compound Interest is Calculated

The standard formula for compound interest with additional monthly contributions is:

A = P × (1 + r/n)n×t + PMT × [((1 + r/n)n×t - 1) / (r/n)] × (1 + r/n)

Where A = future value, P = principal, r = annual interest rate, n = compounding frequency per year, t = number of years, PMT = monthly contribution. Our calculator handles both the initial lump sum and recurring investments.

📐 Understanding Your Results

  • Total Principal Invested: Sum of initial principal plus all monthly contributions.
  • Total Interest Earned: The amount of compound interest generated over the period.
  • Future Value: The final value of your investment including all contributions and compounded interest.

Regular monthly contributions significantly boost the final corpus, especially over long periods, due to the power of compounding.

📜 The Power of Compounding

Albert Einstein reportedly called compound interest the "eighth wonder of the world." The concept dates back to ancient civilizations, but it became central to modern finance with the growth of banking and investment markets. Today, compound interest calculators empower investors to visualize the extraordinary growth potential of disciplined, long-term investing.
