📖 Income Tax in India – Old vs New Regime

For FY 2025-26, individuals can choose between:

  • Old Regime: Higher tax rates but allows deductions under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5L), 80D (health insurance), HRA, 80E (education loan), etc.
  • New Regime: Lower tax rates with no deductions (except standard deduction of ₹50,000 for salaried). Default regime now.

Our calculator computes tax as per the latest rates including surcharge (if taxable income > ₹50 lakh) and 4% health & education cess.

📊 Tax Slabs (FY 2025-26)

Old Regime: 0% up to ₹2.5L, 5% (2.5L-5L), 20% (5L-10L), 30% above 10L. Rebate u/s 87A for income up to ₹5L (tax nil).

New Regime: 0% up to ₹3L, 5% (3L-7L), 10% (7L-10L), 15% (10L-12L), 20% (12L-15L), 30% above 15L. Rebate up to ₹7L (tax nil).

Surcharge: 10% on income ₹50L-1Cr, 15% on ₹1Cr-2Cr, 25% on ₹2Cr-5Cr, 37% above ₹5Cr.
